About ResumeIQ

How the analysis works

ResumeIQ reads your resume the way an experienced editor would — then turns that read into a score and a concrete action list.

1

You paste or upload

Paste your resume text, or drop a .txt / .pdf file. PDF text is extracted right in your browser.

2

AI reads it like an editor

Your resume text is sent securely to our backend, which asks an AI model to grade it against a fixed rubric.

3

You get a rubric, not just a number

Content & Impact, Formatting & Clarity, ATS Friendliness, and Keywords & Relevance — each scored and explained.

4

You get a fix list

Strengths, weaknesses, and a short list of the highest-impact changes to make first.
